# Org routing fixes: who owns a session, how messages reach a role, and hiring the chief and the assistant

Ashot asked for these on Oct 2, 2026, after a week of running the Union workspace on roles. Each fix below comes from a real failure in that workspace; the evidence is named so you can read it. Build them as one coherent change, reusing what exists. Do not add parallel paths.

## 1. A session nobody bound stays with its person

Today a session with no active task or plan belongs to whichever project's `project_path` exactly equals the folder it runs in (`packages/convex/convex/org.ts` `sessionsInScope`, `packages/convex/convex/lib/orgOwnership.ts` `sessionWork` and `projectsListedBy`). In Union this sent every unbound session in Ashot's `~/src/union-mobile/outreach` folder, and every Aivery session on the agent box, to the Calling lead. Callers & Call Management happened to be created with Ashot's path, and camerons ideas with `/Users/ec2-user/src/union-mobile`. The other nine projects were created from Samvit's machine, so they catch nothing. The Calling lead hit its daily token limit three days in one week reading cold email, mailbox, domain and documentation sessions.

The rule Ashot chose: folder matching no longer gives a role ownership. A role holds a session only when the session is bound to a task or plan in its scope, or a person (or their assistant) files it under the role. An unbound session stays with the person who started it. Their Executive Assistant (section 4) files it by what it is about, reading it the way the agent already reads content, not by pattern.

- Remove path matching from `sessionsInScope` and `sessionWork`, and from any other reader of the same rule (search for `project_path` in org code). Keep one definition of the rule in `contracts/orgLead` and `lib/orgOwnership`.
- A project's folder link stays as metadata (new sessions may start there, the project page may show it) but it decides no ownership.
- Make the folder link visible and editable: a `--dir` (or `--path` / `--no-path`) option on `cast project update`, the same field on `projects.update` and `webUpdate` in `packages/convex/convex/projects.ts`, and the field on the project settings page. Today nothing can change it after creation.
- Migration: sessions a role took only by path go back to their person. Then clear the folder links on Callers & Call Management and camerons ideas in the Union workspace (Ashot approved this as decision sd-338).

## 2. Health says where a role's load comes from

The overload alert said "11 sessions at once, 15 threads stuck, 3 days at its limit" and nothing about why. Have the health check and the role brief (`orgHealth.ts`, the brief renderer) say how each session reached the role: bound to a task, bound to a plan, or filed by a person or assistant. An overload then names its source.

## 3. Messages to a role carry their sender and are really delivered

Two failures, both from the Chief of Staff session `jx79zjy`:
- **No sender.** `cast role wake @handle "<text>"` lands in the role's standing session as a plain user turn. `cast send` wraps the same text as `<session-message from="<short id>">`. With no sender, six leads read "send me only what needs Ashot" as their own human and never replied to the chief; one treated the text as untrusted pasted content. Evidence: `cast search "Your area reads stuck" -s 5d`.
- **False success, silent drop.** The Agent Quality lead's standing session (`jx73kh0`) runs on Jason's laptop. `cast role wake` and then `cast send --wake` both printed success, and codecast later dropped both with "no live daemon" while the session kept running its own daily check.

Fix:
- A role wake from a session is delivered exactly like `cast send` from that session, reusing `packages/cli/src/sessionSendCommand.ts`'s sender resolution. A wake from a person in a terminal carries their name (the existing `name=` form). Scheduled wakes the system fires (daily check, area alerts) keep their framing.
- Delivery is honest: a send or wake reports success only once the target has taken the message. When the target's daemon is down, it is queued and delivered when that daemon returns, and the sender sees "queued" rather than "sent". It is never silently dropped. If that is out of reach, at minimum print the failure at the time of sending.
- Role instructions say, at principle level, that a wake from a session is a colleague's message and a requested result goes back with `cast send`.

Entry points: `packages/convex/convex/orgRoles.ts` (`wake` mutation, around line 1857), `packages/cli/src/daemon.ts` (injection), `packages/web/components/sessionMessage.ts` (rendering), `packages/evals/src/surfaces/roleWake/` (extend: "lead replies to the waking session").

## 4. The Chief of Staff and the Executive Assistant can be hired from the gallery

- The Chief of Staff is a special seat hired only through `cast org staff` and the "Hire a Chief of Staff" button on an empty org page (`docs/architecture/org-staffing.md` S6, S12). Once a workspace has roles, a person cannot find it. List it in the hire gallery (`packages/web/components/org/orgTemplateHire.tsx`, `TemplateGallery`, `templateCatalog`) beside templates, shown as hired when present.
- Add the Executive Assistant seat: one per person, reusing `orgRoles.staff`'s path. Name "Executive Assistant", handle `executive-assistant`, reports to the hiring person, lives in their personal workspace, trust understand, starts no work on its own, a daily routine (not a company review). Its job: keep that person's own work in order across every workspace they belong to, meaning the tasks, projects, plans and sessions they started or own that no role holds, including the unbound sessions from section 1. It hires no roles and never reorganizes the company. Its charter sits next to the chief's in `packages/shared/contracts/chiefOfStaffPrompt.ts` and `orgRoles.ts`, generalized from Ashot's live proposal op-51 in his personal workspace.
- Its brief and health read the person's work across all their workspaces; a personal-workspace role otherwise sees an empty scope.
- CLI peer to match the chief: a sibling of `cast org staff`.
- Staffing stays a person's act; the gallery hire posts a proposal like the chief's.

## 5. Small, found on the way

- A role's daily check never reads the initiatives it owns, so health updates go stale (in-1 read "at risk" for 11 days). Include owned initiatives in the role's brief and daily check, so it posts a health update when it has news.
- `cast state show` on a lead's session now refuses for the chief ("only sessions you run or own"), though the chief's charter is to look after those leads. A role should be able to read the state of sessions in roles that report through it.
