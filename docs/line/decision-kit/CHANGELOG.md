# Decision page changelog

- Round 1: present.md now sizes the page to the decision around one real conversation instead of a nine-part checklist, makes producing the after by running the branch the presenter's job, makes the page title the question and keeps answers on the card only (routing words no longer leak into answer text), puts facts against the recommendation at full weight and makes numbers say what they counted, and asks for Union's parts by what a contact sees; the kit README and example drop the default stat strip and the closing answers block, quotes stop reading as headlines (file text goes in a new `pre` style), and limits in a `dd` keep full weight. Why: the critique found one fixed skeleton on every page, placeholder afters, answers shown three times, embellished or demoted facts, and internal names, all traceable to those instructions and the kit template.
- Round 2: present.md now has one frame: say each thing once, where its weight earns it, and lead with what would most change the founder's answer. The deciding fact (often one against the lean) shapes the question, opens the context and sits beside its evidence. The recommendation is argued only in the context, and the question asks without arguing. The page no longer repeats the question; its title says what happened to people, and it grows outward from one conversation with no list of sections to fill. A number is shown at the strength of its count, and nothing uncounted becomes a figure. The after is what the person would meet (a message, a booking, silence), rendered as the conversation continuing, never as tool output. The diff is the few deciding lines plus which case they govern. The decision is posted once and edited afterwards. In the kit, the README template and example drop the question-as-H1, the recommendation lead and the Proof/Reach/Risk table, and facts are reserved for counted numbers. The kit renders a message's markdown bold and links, folds messages by length instead of measured height, and drops the line-number chips and word-count fold labels. Why: round 2's three pages shared one skeleton, put deciding facts in the weakest slot, overstated numbers, showed JSON and log lines as the after, and printed literal asterisks; each traced to one of those instructions or to kit behaviour.
- Round 3: present.md is reframed from presenting a fix to bringing the founder the judgment under it (how Union should treat a person here), so the question is where a founder could disagree, the fix's premise is never stated as settled, answers are positions a founder could take (including ones the fix does not build) with required checks as conditions of the recommendation, and evidence that argues with itself is reconciled in the open; the after now keeps everything the change does not control, dates come from received timestamps and judging words (on time, kept) must be settled against the exact terms, reach across Union is counted with a light query or named as unknown in the context, quoted internals get a gloss, and the page title and `<title>` are one wording. In the kit, paired facts must count the same thing the same way (README and example no longer model a production count against a replay count), `<title>` matches the h1, the shared height shim (packages/shared/render/pageTheme.ts) no longer measures a frame that is not laid out yet (a background tab loaded it at 0x0 and posted ~122k to 169k px), and the replay preview lists answers one per row as prod does. Why: round 3's page contradicted its own dates and after, defaulted to ship/rework/drop with housekeeping as the middle answer, asserted the point under decision, set unlike counts side by side, left reach uncounted, and rendered a 122k px blank; scores had stalled for three rounds, which pointed at the prompt's framing rather than missing rules.
- Round 4: present.md now ties the recommendation to the evidence (a fact shown against the lean lowers it or becomes the question; conditions are only for owed mechanical checks, never a parked doubt), makes the after the presenter's job for every case shown and above all the risky one (a summary of a run is not an after; an unproducible deciding after becomes the question), reads question, context and page as one document where the page continues from the context and never restates it (page `<title>`/`<h1>` are the question), drops answers the record shows were already tried, asks for the record's own vocabulary to be translated, and checks that a count's name is true of what it caught; the prompt stays the same length. The kit hides the page `<h1>` when framed under the card, wraps a `dk-event`'s children so mixed text and `<time>` read as one line (long events read as a full-size sentence, not a grey caption), the example drops its lede, top stat strip, summary `dl` and closing answers section, the README makes a decisive `dk-gap` the question rather than a box, and the replay preview sizes canvas frames to their body (no dead space). Why: round 4 recommended past its own evidence with conditions, showed summaries or dashed boxes where the after belonged, told each point two or three times under a new headline, copied the example's skeleton, and rendered events as squeezed columns; scores sat at 6.1 to 6.5 for four rounds, so the framing (lean then disclose, hero-only after, page as a second telling) was changed rather than added to.
