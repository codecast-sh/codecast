# Decision page changelog

- Round 1: present.md now sizes the page to the decision around one real conversation instead of a nine-part checklist, makes producing the after by running the branch the presenter's job, makes the page title the question and keeps answers on the card only (routing words no longer leak into answer text), puts facts against the recommendation at full weight and makes numbers say what they counted, and asks for Union's parts by what a contact sees; the kit README and example drop the default stat strip and the closing answers block, quotes stop reading as headlines (file text goes in a new `pre` style), and limits in a `dd` keep full weight. Why: the critique found one fixed skeleton on every page, placeholder afters, answers shown three times, embellished or demoted facts, and internal names, all traceable to those instructions and the kit template.
- Round 2: present.md now has one frame: say each thing once, where its weight earns it, and lead with what would most change the founder's answer. The deciding fact (often one against the lean) shapes the question, opens the context and sits beside its evidence. The recommendation is argued only in the context, and the question asks without arguing. The page no longer repeats the question; its title says what happened to people, and it grows outward from one conversation with no list of sections to fill. A number is shown at the strength of its count, and nothing uncounted becomes a figure. The after is what the person would meet (a message, a booking, silence), rendered as the conversation continuing, never as tool output. The diff is the few deciding lines plus which case they govern. The decision is posted once and edited afterwards. In the kit, the README template and example drop the question-as-H1, the recommendation lead and the Proof/Reach/Risk table, and facts are reserved for counted numbers. The kit renders a message's markdown bold and links, folds messages by length instead of measured height, and drops the line-number chips and word-count fold labels. Why: round 2's three pages shared one skeleton, put deciding facts in the weakest slot, overstated numbers, showed JSON and log lines as the after, and printed literal asterisks; each traced to one of those instructions or to kit behaviour.
- Round 3: present.md is reframed from presenting a fix to bringing the founder the judgment under it (how Union should treat a person here), so the question is where a founder could disagree, the fix's premise is never stated as settled, answers are positions a founder could take (including ones the fix does not build) with required checks as conditions of the recommendation, and evidence that argues with itself is reconciled in the open; the after now keeps everything the change does not control, dates come from received timestamps and judging words (on time, kept) must be settled against the exact terms, reach across Union is counted with a light query or named as unknown in the context, quoted internals get a gloss, and the page title and `<title>` are one wording. In the kit, paired facts must count the same thing the same way (README and example no longer model a production count against a replay count), `<title>` matches the h1, the shared height shim (packages/shared/render/pageTheme.ts) no longer measures a frame that is not laid out yet (a background tab loaded it at 0x0 and posted ~122k to 169k px), and the replay preview lists answers one per row as prod does. Why: round 3's page contradicted its own dates and after, defaulted to ship/rework/drop with housekeeping as the middle answer, asserted the point under decision, set unlike counts side by side, left reach uncounted, and rendered a 122k px blank; scores had stalled for three rounds, which pointed at the prompt's framing rather than missing rules.
- Round 4: present.md now ties the recommendation to the evidence (a fact shown against the lean lowers it or becomes the question; conditions are only for owed mechanical checks, never a parked doubt), makes the after the presenter's job for every case shown and above all the risky one (a summary of a run is not an after; an unproducible deciding after becomes the question), reads question, context and page as one document where the page continues from the context and never restates it (page `<title>`/`<h1>` are the question), drops answers the record shows were already tried, asks for the record's own vocabulary to be translated, and checks that a count's name is true of what it caught; the prompt stays the same length. The kit hides the page `<h1>` when framed under the card, wraps a `dk-event`'s children so mixed text and `<time>` read as one line (long events read as a full-size sentence, not a grey caption), the example drops its lede, top stat strip, summary `dl` and closing answers section, the README makes a decisive `dk-gap` the question rather than a box, and the replay preview sizes canvas frames to their body (no dead space). Why: round 4 recommended past its own evidence with conditions, showed summaries or dashed boxes where the after belonged, told each point two or three times under a new headline, copied the example's skeleton, and rendered events as squeezed columns; scores sat at 6.1 to 6.5 for four rounds, so the framing (lean then disclose, hero-only after, page as a second telling) was changed rather than added to.
- Round 5: present.md now makes establishing whether the code the branch edits still runs today (origin/main, a light production count, what replaced it) the presenter's first duty, and makes a dead path the deciding fact; the after is run on the path that reaches people today, with and without the change; the context opens with the deciding fact and the recommendation and holds only what twenty seconds can; the page opens on the deciding fact's evidence and shows only the stretch of the thread that bears on the decision; the final read-through checks every word is the founder's and every claim is true of Union today. The kit sets the h1 (the question, a sentence) at reading size instead of a 21ch display headline. Why: both round 5 pages built their story on prompts main retired on Sep 22 (one argued its main harm and two answers from a dead path, the other buried that fact in section four and left the live path's after as "not run"), opened on a whole thread before the deciding fact, put the recommendation in paragraph five or six of a ~380-word context, and drew the question as a four to six line display headline; the prompt never asked whether the edited code was live, never ordered the context, and named a whole conversation as the page's heart.
- Round 6: present.md now opens the page where the deciding fact meets a person (one real conversation) and puts how anything was counted after what it counts, at the size that convinces; shows the change as the words its reader meets (the instructions as the model receives them, rendered for the hero's case, with the template source kept below) plus any unchanged line that still pulls against it; runs before and after the same way so the change is the only difference; reads every replayed output against everything the changed instruction and Union ask of it, not only the problem, with every counted run on the page beside its case; makes a figure and its detail one reading (a worse detail means the figure is wrong); asks for the evidence that would settle a position the record leaves open; has each answer say what happens next; and names the machinery's words as the thing to translate. The kit README makes the rendered before/after text the default diff (pasted `git diff` for code and for files that are already the words), quotes unchanged lines that pull against the change under it, and the kit styles a closed `details` for the source. Why: round 6's headline table counted one untrue statement where its own prose showed two, never checked replays against the 50-word limit the rewrite keeps, opened on method and fifteen near-identical lines before the hero, showed a raw nunjucks diff of 400-character conditionals, compared a real email to replays signed by another sender, counted replays it never showed, and let "broker" through; each traced to the prompt's page order, its "as git printed it" diff rule, an after compared only on the problem's axis, and summary numbers written apart from the detail. The page h1 repeating the card question is a source-reading artifact: the kit hides it when framed.
- Round 7: present.md now picks the question from the deciding fact (the question that fact leaves open may not be about the fix, and the lean follows the fact, not the branch), names the record's own limits (what the reviewer says was not verified, a cause the investigation named that the build may have left in place, stations counting one thing differently) as the tension to carry at full weight, states the context as a goal (the case across a desk in twenty seconds, form following content, a canvas where counts read faster as a picture) instead of a list of parts, sizes the page to the change's idea instead of an ordered sequence, drops any case it cannot give an after, keeps a flaw the change leaves as it was to a sentence, and applies plain words to everything the founder reads, quoted model outputs and answers included (the routing line no longer invites "the builder" into answers); same length. The kit diffs before/after templates line by line (a pure addition shows only the added lines under a dim anchor line, a mostly kept line is marked inline), drops the +N/-N counters, and styles plain tables; the README says a call line's time is its place in the call and that both sides of a diff are pasted whole. Why: round 7's context turned the prompt's parts list into four bold slots with ten numbers and no canvas, asked about the note while its own deciding fact said coaching two callers was the lever, dropped the reviewer's unverified replay and the unchanged legacy grader, showed an un-rerun case and a fee detour, passed "rung", "draw" and "Builder" through, and rendered a pure addition as Was and Now with counters and placeholder boxes beside an unstyled table; scores fell after round 6, so the framing of the context and question was changed rather than added to.
- Round 8: present.md is reframed around layers the founder may stop at (the first lines say what goes wrong, what was tried and what you would do; the card alone is enough to decide on; the page shows the thing), with every layer growing with the decision rather than the data; the context opens on the answer and the fact behind it, anyone named is introduced where first named, and method (models, draws, run and finding ids) stays in the pinned fields; every answer carries what the record says about it and the figure that sizes it, and the recommended course is shown in its own words (drafted and, where cheap, run) when it is not the branch as built; evidence is shown at the size of its point; a cause is a finding only when something isolated it; each check is applied to every output alike; Ship and Drop read as the label's own first verb; same length (1,872 words). The kit gives the page a `<header>` (question plus bottom line) shown only when the page is read alone, adds `brief` messages that show only their marked paragraphs, frames a side's non-message outcome as its own slot instead of a divider, and draws every prose diff in one convention (struck, lit, plain) with no Was/Now/Added/Removed/After tags and a caption in words rather than mono; the replay harness saves report.html as served (kit inline) with the written page beside it as report.source.html. Why: round 8's context reached its recommendation in paragraph three with unintroduced names and judge/replay vocabulary, the recommended rework and the alternative answer had no shown wording or evidence, twelve full drafts made a 9,800 px page, a causal story rested on three draws while the same check was applied to one name and not another, and the kit's mixed diff labels, divider-like "sent nothing" events and unstyled raw report each traced to the prompt's answer, context and "every run on the page" instructions or to kit and harness behaviour; scores sat at 6.3 to 7 for eight rounds, so the framing (what a reader who stops early must have) changed instead of rules being added.
- Round 9: present.md now makes the question about how Union should treat people today, never about the branch (a retired or missed path makes dropping the branch one answer, not the question), with every answer answering it; makes the presenter the founder's skeptic who runs every check it can before posting and offers an unbuilt or unrun course as work still to do, never as the recommendation to adopt; splits the card (read in a glance, on a phone, in the app's monospace: question, answers as a label plus one clause on what happens next, a few lines of context with only the figure the deciding fact needs) from the page (all evidence); makes a number carry every correction wherever it appears (reach counts only moments the change acts on, on paths production still runs, and a narrowed count replaces the raw one in the first lines too); widens plain words to branches, checks and template fields, glossed even inside quoted text; and says the text the decision turns on is shown whole. The kit never folds a message inside a `dk-side` (the README no longer offers `brief` for drafts), wraps code diff lines instead of scrolling them, colours a `mark` red only inside the message at fault and a neutral highlight elsewhere, and drops fact tones. Why: round 9's answers were stat paragraphs because the prompt asked each for its figure and record, the improvised recommendation came from the instruction to write and run a recommended course that was not the branch, "Drop the fix?" came from picking the question by the deciding fact rather than by the judgment, headline figures kept raw counts the page later narrowed because no instruction carried a caveat upward, and the truncated afters, clipped rule line, red underlines on good text and green absences were the README's `brief` guidance and the kit's no-wrap rows, single mark colour and tone colours; with scores flat for nine rounds, the card and page were given different jobs instead of adding rules.
- Round 10: present.md reframes the context from the case for your answer to the decision in small (what you would do and the fact that most stands against it, at the page's own strength and number, every reason checked against the page's cases), so the card can no longer be advocacy over a page of disclosure; adopting holds only if it would survive the checks still owed, otherwise those checks are the answer; answers come from what the page found (the narrowest course that keeps what works and removes each shown weakness belongs among them), with Ship and Drop as verbs, never labels; each after is read whole as its recipient would before asking whether it fixed the problem, so what it newly says, offers or invents counts as the change, and silence is shown as what the person is left hearing; each thing appears once across card and page (the change once, an after continues the conversation already shown, runs that repeat are counted, method adds only what cases do not say), one name per thing. The README drops the closed template source under the rendered diff. The kit strikes deleted words in solid red over dimmed text, also inside code lines. Why: round 10's tops shrank weaknesses the bodies admitted because the context was framed as making the case; the missing narrower answer and Ship/alternative/Drop sets came from deriving answers from the branch's fate; replays were graded only on the problem's metric; the triple-told change, twice-shown email, six near-identical replays and re-listed cases traced to the "source kept below", "a sentence on each changed line" and "every run on the page" instructions; and a 1px translucent strike was invisible in dark mode, so mixed lines read as garble.
