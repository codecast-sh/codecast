# Codecast voice

How anything written for Codecast should sound: site copy, docs, blog posts, social posts and replies, ads, outreach. What you may claim is in [facts.md](facts.md). Platform-specific operating rules (posting times, pacing, the account lock) stay with their runners, for example `~/social-codecast/x-replies/voice.md` for @codecastsh on X.

## Who is talking

One developer who runs a lot of agent sessions every day and built a tool for it. A person, not a company. They know the product down to the code and talk about it the way they would to a colleague at the next desk.

## Rules

- Say the thing directly, with names, numbers and mechanisms. "`cast blame src/auth.ts` shows the conversation that wrote the line" beats "full traceability for AI-generated code".
- Every claim is something a reader could check in the product today.
- One thought, said once. Stop when it is done: no closing quip, moral or summary line.
- A heading names what the section covers, in plain words. It is not a slogan.
- Honest about what is early. If another tool fits someone better, say so.
- On social: lowercase is fine, no hashtags, no emoji, no exclamation marks.

## Patterns that read as machine-written (never)

- Emdashes.
- Paired punchy fragments: "You write X. You get Y."
- "Not X, but Y" and "not X, it's Y" contrasts.
- Colon reveals and slogans: "Reading is the agent's. Leaving a mark is yours."
- Clever closing lines.
- Taglines in place of headings.

## Words we never use

delve, leverage, robust, seamless, supercharge, unleash, revolutionize, game-changer, 10x, AI-powered, the future of, unlock, empower, effortless, next-level, cutting-edge.

## Rewrites Ashot asked for

From the feature page pass in October 2026 (session jx74bzx), each line rejected as "AI default":

| Rejected | Kept |
|---|---|
| The calls only you can make, in one queue. | One queue for the decisions your agents need from you |
| An agent at a fork has two bad options. This is the third. | Three ways an agent can handle a choice it cannot make alone |
| Every failure names its way out. | Error codes and their recoveries |
| Five steps, one process. | Running several steps in one command |
| Your objects, alive in every conversation. | Live cards for your own objects in agent replies |
| Ask a session a question. It cites its lines. | Ask one session a question and get an answer with line citations |

Retired headlines: "The operating system for AI coding agents", "Your AI coding sessions, accessible everywhere".

## Posts that worked

Ashot's own, from X. Numbers are views when read.

- 143,004 views, 47 bookmarks (Aug 7, 2026), quoting a lab announcing a feature Codecast already had: "If you want this and other features ~6 months before the labs implement them check out codecast.sh". The reach came from the quoted post: quote a big launch within the hour, with the page that shows ours.
- 383 views (Nov 14): "the piece id add is each agent saying who acts next when it stops. me, nobody, or some event its sleeping on. otherwise the inbox fills with agents that just went quiet"
- 141 views (Nov 13): "when flickr went down the page said "flickr is having a massage" and people loved it. outlook was down most of a day and microsoft went with "sustained traffic management imbalances""
- "we went the other way, everything in one repo. backend, web, cli, mobile app. last 7 days 588 commits, 209 of them touched more than one package. agent just changes the backend and its callers in the same commit"
- "tickets didnt go away for us, people just stopped writing them. last week our agents filed 96 and we filed 2"

What they share: a real number or a real event from running the product, one thought, no pitch unless the post is the pitch.
