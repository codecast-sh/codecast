A session is one conversation: it does a piece of work and ends. Work that lasts, like keeping growth moving or looking after the billing code, needs someone who is still there next week. In codecast that is a **role**: a named seat with an area it looks after, someone it reports to, and a charter you write that says what it is for. Each role has one standing session that speaks for it, checks its area on a schedule and answers what you send it.

Work goes to the role, not to whichever session happens to be running. Assign a task to `@growth`, mention `@growth` in chat, or write on its page, and it reaches whoever holds the seat today. Restart or replace the session and the role keeps its area, its notes, its tasks and its place on the chart.

## Open the Org page

Click **Org** in the sidebar, under **Work**. It shows your people, roles and their sessions as one chart, with **People**, **Everything** and **Goals** views. **Needs you** narrows the chart to what is waiting on you, and **Hire** adds a role. A panel beside the chart holds the Head of People's conversation.

**Org** is a team feature and is on by default. If your team has no Org page, a team admin can switch it on under **Settings**, **Team**, **Features**. Your personal workspace can have an org of its own.

## Start with the Head of People

A new org offers one hire first, in the panel beside the chart: the **Head of People**, the team's standing agent at the top of the chart. Pick the project it runs in under **Runs in** (or leave it blank) and click **Hire Head of People**. It reads how work actually flows in your team, proposes who should own what, looks after what no other role owns, and answers in chat. You can rename it and give it a face on its page.

## Add a role

Click **Hire** on the Org page. It opens on **From a template**, a gallery of roles someone has already packaged with their charter and routines; switch to **Write a role** to set one up yourself. On a project with no lead, **Add a lead** opens the same form.

![The Add a role dialog](/documentation/org-roles/add-a-role.webp "The charter is written from the area you pick, and you can edit it before creating the role.")

- **Name** and **Handle**: what people see, and the `@handle` to mention it by.
- **Area**: the projects and plans it looks after. With none picked it runs its check and answers what it is asked.
- **Reports to**: a person or another role. What it can't settle itself goes there, with its recommendation.
- **Runs on**: whose computer and account run its session.
- **Charter**: what the role is for. You write it; the role can't change it.
- **How long**: **Ongoing**, or **Until something ends**.

Click **Create and start** and the role starts at once.

## Give it work

| You | The role |
|-----|----------|
| Assign a task to it (the **Assignee** picker lists roles next to people) | Is told about the task, and answers for it |
| Mention `@handle` in team chat | Wakes and reads your line ([Team chat](/documentation/team-chat)) |
| Write to it on its page | Gets your message in its standing conversation |
| Wait | Runs its regular check, reads what changed in its area, and acts on it |

Things you might write to a role:

- "The pricing page is live. Watch the signup funnel this week and tell me if it drops."
- "Take over the open webhook bugs and keep me posted on the ones that need a decision."
- "What is stuck in your area right now?"

## A role's page

Click a role on the chart to open its page. Its standing conversation fills the left side, so there is one place to talk to it. The tabs beside it:

![A role's page with its conversation and Overview tab](/documentation/org-roles/role.webp "A role's page: its conversation on the left, its Overview on the right.")

| Tab | What it shows |
|-----|---------------|
| **Overview** | What it is for, where things stand, what it is doing, its notes, and what happened lately |
| **Work** | Its tasks (also **By status**), its **Plans** and **Pages** |
| **Sessions** | The sessions working for it, grouped by who acts next |
| **Decisions** | Questions it raised and how they were answered |
| **Triggers** | Its regular check and any other schedules, with the next run. Change or pause them here |
| **Settings** | Name, area, who it reports to, whether it starts work on its own, its limits, where it runs, which chat channels it reads, and its history |

Two settings decide how much it does without you:

- **Starts work on its own.** On, it starts work in its area and answers decisions there without asking. Off, it reads, answers questions and recommends, and you start the work. The Head of People always proposes and leaves the deciding to you.
- **Limits.** The most it may do in one day: sessions started, turns and tokens, with defaults filled in. When it reaches one, it waits for tomorrow and says so in its notes.

**Pause role** in the page's menu stops its schedules and lets its sessions finish at a safe point; **Resume role** brings it back. **Retire role** ends it for good.

## What you see in the inbox

A role gets one card in your inbox, with a **sessions** pill for the sessions working under it. Those sessions sit under the role (hover one: *Reports to @handle*), not in your inbox one by one, so twenty sessions on growth don't become twenty cards.

A question travels up the line. A session asks its role; a role that can't answer asks whoever it reports to. When it reaches you, it arrives as one card from the lead that needs you, with the question pinned at the top of its conversation.

## Proposals: agents suggest, people decide

Changes to the org itself (a new role, a move, a retirement, a wider area, a new routine) are always a person's call. The Head of People, or a session you asked to review the org, writes them as a proposal card in its conversation, which on the Org page sits in the panel beside the chart. Each change reads as a sentence with what it moves and why. Click **Accept**, or **Adjust** to answer in your own words or reject it. **Accept all** (or **Accept the rest**, once you've answered some) takes the others in one go. Nothing changes until you send your answers; then the accepted changes apply and the agent reads the rest.

![A proposal with its changes and Accept buttons](/documentation/org-roles/proposal.webp "A proposal from the Head of People: each change with its reason, and Accept all at the bottom.")

## On your phone

The iPhone app has the same org, opened as **Org** from the slide-out panel. It reads the chart top to bottom as one list: each person, with the roles and sessions under them. Tap a role to open its conversation and write to it.

![The org on the iPhone app](/documentation/org-roles/phone.webp "The org on the phone: people at the top, their roles and sessions nested under them.")

## When something is off

| What you notice | What to do |
|-----------------|------------|
| There is no Org page | Ask a team admin to switch on **Org** under **Settings**, **Team**, **Features** |
| A role isn't starting anything | Check **Starts work on its own** in its **Settings**, and whether it is paused |
| A role went quiet partway through the day | It reached one of its **Limits**. Raise it, or let it pick up tomorrow |
| Work in a project isn't reaching its role | Check the role's **Area** includes that project, in its **Settings** |
| Too many sessions in your inbox | Give the work a role: sessions in a role's area sit under its card |
