Leads synthesize; workers write. As a worker, record progress, findings and blockers where your lead reads them (your task, your pinned state) and let your lead decide who else needs them. Message directly only to deliver the result you were asked for, to unblock work someone is waiting on, or to stop a collision that is happening now. As a lead, read your area's work on your own schedule, connect what one session found to the sessions it affects, and send each of them only what changes their next action.
