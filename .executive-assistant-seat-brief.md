Make the Executive Assistant and the Chief of Staff hireable from the org page's hire gallery.

## Why
Ashot asked (Oct 1) to hire a Chief of Staff for himself and could not find it in the hire gallery. Two things are missing:
1. The Chief of Staff is a special seat hired only through `cast org staff` and the "Hire a Chief of Staff" button on an empty org page (docs/architecture/org-staffing.md S6, S12). Once a workspace has roles, nothing in the gallery offers it, so a person cannot find it.
2. What he actually wanted is a different job: an Executive Assistant that keeps one person's own unowned work in order (their tasks, projects, plans and sessions that no lead looks after, across every workspace they belong to), hires no roles, and never reorganizes the company. Folder templates (org-templates.md) cannot express this: a template installs one role for exactly one project.

## Goal
- The hire gallery (packages/web/components/org/orgTemplateHire.tsx, TemplateGallery, templateCatalog) lists built-in seats beside templates: Chief of Staff (one per workspace; shown as hired when present) and Executive Assistant (one per person).
- Executive Assistant is a seat like the chief's, reusing orgRoles.staff's path rather than a parallel one: name "Executive Assistant", handle `executive-assistant`, reports to the hiring person, lives in their personal workspace, trust understand, starts no work on its own, a daily routine (not a company review). Its charter lives next to the Chief of Staff's charter template (packages/shared/contracts/chiefOfStaffPrompt.ts and orgRoles.ts) and is generalized from the one in Ashot's live proposal op-51 (personal workspace), with his name replaced by the hiring person's.
- Its brief and health read the person's own work across all their workspaces (owned or started by them, not in any role's scope), since a personal-workspace role otherwise sees an empty scope.
- The CLI peer: `cast org staff --seat-kind assistant` or a sibling command, matching how the chief is hired.

## Done means
Unit tests for the seat (idempotent per person, never touches team scopes), the gallery showing both seats, an end-to-end hire in a throwaway account, and `cast check` green. Leave the change in the main checkout's working tree and report in this thread.
